# Buyer FAQ

- **What do I pay online?** Only the small platform fee, to book the order.
- **When do I pay for the food?** At your door, to the kitchen's UPI (or cash for pickup), after the food arrives.
- **Can I cancel?** Before the kitchen accepts, the fee is refunded. After it accepts, the fee is kept.
- **The kitchen says I did not pay.** Report it from the order; PadosiPlate reviews and decides.
- **Delete my account?** Account → delete. It is blocked while an order is open.

## New in Pilot v2

- **Can I order for later?** Yes: choose “Order for later” at checkout. You pay the small platform fee now and the kitchen at your door.
- **How do I follow a kitchen?** Tap the heart; choose whether you want a message when it cooks.
- **Who can read my chat?** You and the kitchen. An admin reads it only if a report or complaint needs it, with a reason, and it is logged. Chats close 48 hours after delivery.
- **What if I disagree with a complaint decision or a strike?** Appeal from Account → Strikes, or write to the grievance officer from the Grievance page.
