# Kitchen guide

1. Join through your society's invite link and finish the onboarding wizard.
2. Save your UPI ID and complete the ₹1 check (you pay ₹1 from that ID; we refund it).
3. Use "Help me apply" for FSSAI; apply yourself on the official portal and never share your FoSCoS password with anyone.
4. Add dishes with allergens and veg/non-veg marks.
5. Accept orders within 20 minutes. Buyers pay you at the door, after the food arrives. Kootu never holds your food money.
6. Declining a booked order refunds the buyer's fee and gives them credit; cancelling after accepting also earns you a strike.

## New in Pilot v2

- **Dish rules**: every dish needs a food mark (veg / egg / non-veg), ingredients and allergens (or “none of these”). Prohibited items are blocked; health claims send a dish to review.
- **Today’s menu**: publish it in one tap; followers who asked are told once a day, and you get a WhatsApp share link.
- **Closed today** pauses new orders without losing your queue; pre-orders for later days still work.
- **Earnings** shows fees and the TDS export; **quality** shows your acceptance rate. Declining a booked order refunds the buyer’s fee and gives them credit, with no strike, but it lowers your acceptance rate.
